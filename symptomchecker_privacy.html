Privacy Policy — Dr. Shah Health Assistant

Last updated: September 2026

Dr. Shah Health Assistant ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy, especially given the sensitive nature of this app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Symptom Information. When you use the symptom checker, the body part, symptom description, duration, age, and gender you provide are sent to a third-party AI service (Google Gemini API) to generate a preliminary, informational response. This data is processed to generate your result and is not permanently stored on our servers.

Advertising Data. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not require account creation and do not collect your name, email address, or other personally identifiable information to use this app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above solely to generate a preliminary, non-diagnostic AI response to help you understand your symptoms in general terms and decide whether to seek in-person medical care.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your symptom information is sent to Google's Gemini API solely to generate your informational response. If you choose to tap the WhatsApp contact button, the message you send is delivered directly through WhatsApp to Dr. Muhammad Jahangeer Shah's clinic and is not seen or stored by us.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your symptom entries and AI-generated responses are not stored on our servers after being generated and displayed to you. No history of your entries is retained by this app.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to use the app fully without providing any personal information beyond what is needed to generate your symptom analysis, and to opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

While this app allows selecting "child" as a patient category for a parent or guardian entering symptoms on a child's behalf, the app itself is intended to be operated by an adult and is not directed at children under 13 as its own users. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

7. Important Medical Disclaimer

Dr. Shah Health Assistant provides a preliminary, AI-generated, non-diagnostic response based on the information you enter. It is not a medical diagnosis and is not a substitute for professional medical evaluation, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult Dr. Muhammad Jahangeer Shah or another qualified healthcare provider for your health concerns. If you or someone with you is experiencing a medical emergency — such as difficulty breathing, facial or throat swelling, or a severe reaction — do not rely on this app; go to the nearest emergency room or contact your local emergency number immediately.

8. Third-Party Services

This app uses Google Gemini API and Google AdMob, each governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
