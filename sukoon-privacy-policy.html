Privacy Policy — Sukoon

Last updated: August 2026

Sukoon ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy, especially given the sensitive nature of this app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Screening Responses. When you complete a mental wellness screening (Depression, Anxiety, or Stress), your responses are used to calculate your results and are sent to a third-party AI service (Google Gemini API) to generate a supportive, informational report. This data is processed to generate your report and is not permanently stored on our servers.

Language Preference. We store your selected language (English or Urdu) locally on your device.

Advertising Data. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not require or collect any account information, email addresses, or personal contact details to use this app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to calculate your screening results, generate a supportive, non-diagnostic informational report via AI, remember your language preference, display relevant advertisements, and improve app stability.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your screening responses are sent to Google's Gemini API solely to generate your informational report. We share data only with Google Gemini API and Google AdMob.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your screening responses and results are not stored on our servers after your report is generated. We do not retain any record of individual screening results or the sensitive nature of your responses.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to use the app fully without providing any personal information, retake a screening at any time, and opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for adult users and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Important Medical Disclaimer

Sukoon is a screening tool that provides general educational and informational content only. It is not a diagnosis and is not a substitute for professional mental health evaluation, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a licensed psychiatrist, psychologist, or qualified healthcare provider regarding your mental health.

If you are experiencing a mental health crisis or having thoughts of self-harm, please contact a crisis helpline or emergency services immediately.

8. Third-Party Services

This app uses Google Gemini API and Google AdMob, each governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
