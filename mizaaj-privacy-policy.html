Privacy Policy — Mizaaj

Last updated: August 2026

Mizaaj ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Quiz Responses. When you take the personality quiz, your answers to the 15 questions are used to determine your personality archetype and are sent to a third-party AI service (Google Gemini API) to generate a personalized analysis. This data is processed to generate your report and is not permanently stored on our servers.

Language Preference. We store your selected language (English or Urdu) locally on your device to remember your preference between sessions.

Advertising Data. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not require or collect any account information, email addresses, or personal contact details to use this app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to generate your personalized personality analysis via AI, remember your language preference, display relevant advertisements, and improve app stability and performance.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your quiz answers are sent to Google's Gemini API solely to generate your personality report. We share data only with Google Gemini API (AI personality analysis) and Google AdMob (advertising).

4. Data Storage & Security

Your quiz answers and results are not stored on our servers after your report is generated. Your language preference is stored locally on your device only.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to use the app fully without providing any personal information, retake the quiz at any time (which discards previous answers), and opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Content Disclaimer

Mizaaj provides personality insights for entertainment and self-reflection purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional psychological assessment or advice. Results are generated based on quiz responses and general personality patterns, and should not be treated as clinical or diagnostic in nature.

8. Third-Party Services

This app uses Google Gemini API and Google AdMob, each governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
