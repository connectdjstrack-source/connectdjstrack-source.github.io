Privacy Policy — SpeechSpark AI

Last updated: August 2026

SpeechSpark AI ("we", "us", "our") is a speech therapy app designed for children ages 1.5–8, made for Dr. Muhammad Jahangeer Shah, Child Development Specialist. We take children's privacy extremely seriously and this Privacy Policy explains, in full, how the app handles information.

This app is designed to be used by children under the direct supervision of a parent or guardian. A parental gate (a simple math question) must be answered correctly before the app's main content becomes accessible, in line with Google Play Families Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Speech Audio (Processed On-Device Only). When a child taps the microphone button to practice a word, the app uses your device's built-in speech recognition to convert speech to text for scoring pronunciation accuracy. This audio is processed using your device's or Google's on-device/system speech recognition service and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted to our servers.

Practice Progress. Words learned, accuracy scores, session streaks, and attempted words are stored locally on your device only, using local device storage. This data is never transmitted off the device and is not accessible to us.

No Account, No Personal Information. SpeechSpark AI does not require account creation, does not collect names, email addresses, birthdates, photos, or any other personally identifiable information about the child or parent.

2. No Advertising, No Analytics, No Third-Party Tracking

This app does not display advertisements. This app does not use analytics or tracking SDKs. This app does not use advertising identifiers. Nothing about your child's usage of this app is shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services Used

The app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech and speech recognition capabilities (provided by the Android operating system) to speak words aloud and to score a child's spoken repetition. These are processed locally by your device's operating system and are governed by your device manufacturer's and Google's own system-level privacy practices, not by us.

4. Data Storage & Security

All progress data (words learned, accuracy, streaks) is stored exclusively on the device using secure local storage. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data. We do not operate any servers that receive or store data from this app.

5. Parental Controls

A parental gate (a simple addition question) is shown before the app's main content is accessible, to help ensure a parent or guardian, not the child alone, is granting access. The Parent Dashboard tab provides insights and session summaries intended for the supervising adult.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

This app is designed for use by children with parental supervision and is built to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy. Because we do not collect any personal information from children, and all data stays on-device, no personal data is ever transmitted, sold, or shared regarding any child user.

7. Content Disclaimer

SpeechSpark AI is an educational speech practice tool for informational and practice purposes only. It is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional speech-language pathology evaluation or treatment. If you have concerns about your child's speech development, please consult Dr. Muhammad Jahangeer Shah or a qualified speech-language pathologist.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
