Privacy Policy — AutismSpark

Last updated: September 2026

AutismSpark ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy, especially given the sensitive nature of this app involving information about children. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Child Information. When you use the screening tool, the child's name (optional), age, gender, family history, parent concern, and your answers to 20 screening questions are sent to a third-party AI service (Google Gemini API) to generate a preliminary, informational screening report. This data is processed to generate your result and is not permanently stored on our servers.

Callback Request Information (Optional). If you choose to request a callback through the app's form, the name, phone number, and email you provide are sent directly via WhatsApp to the clinic and are used solely to facilitate a response. This information is not stored by us.

Advertising Data. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not require account creation and this app does not collect any photos, videos, or audio recordings of your child.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above solely to generate your child's preliminary, AI-based screening report, provide a downloadable PDF report (if unlocked), facilitate optional callback requests, and display relevant advertisements. Watching a rewarded video ad is offered as one way to unlock the full report and PDF, as an alternative to payment.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or your child's information. Screening answers are sent to Google's Gemini API solely to generate your informational report. If you request a callback, your contact details are sent directly to Dr. Muhammad Jahangeer Shah's clinic via WhatsApp for that sole purpose. We share data only with Google Gemini API, Google AdMob, and, only if you opt in, the clinic's WhatsApp contact.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your child's screening answers and generated report are not stored on our servers after being displayed to you. If you download a PDF report, it is saved locally on your own device and is not uploaded or retained by us.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to use the app's core screening without providing your child's name, decline to submit a callback request, retake the screening at any time, and opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is designed to be filled out by a parent or guardian about their child — it is not intended to be used directly by children themselves. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children, and the app does not require the child to interact with it in any way.

7. Important Medical Disclaimer

AutismSpark is an AI-based, M-CHAT-informed preliminary screening tool that provides general educational and informational content only. It is not a medical diagnosis and is not a substitute for professional evaluation by a qualified pediatrician, developmental specialist, or psychologist. The activities, therapy suggestions, and school report generated by this app are general informational guidance, not a prescribed treatment plan. Always consult Dr. Muhammad Jahangeer Shah or another qualified healthcare provider regarding your child's development.

8. Third-Party Services

This app uses Google Gemini API and Google AdMob, each governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
