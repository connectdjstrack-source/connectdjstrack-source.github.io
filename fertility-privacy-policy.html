Privacy Policy — Fertility & Wellness

Last updated: August 2026

Fertility & Wellness ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy, especially given the sensitive nature of this app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Assessment Responses. When you complete the fertility and wellness assessment, your responses to questions about your health and lifestyle are used to calculate your results and are sent to a third-party AI service (Google Gemini API) to generate a supportive, informational wellness report. This data is processed to generate your report and is not permanently stored on our servers.

Lead Capture Information (Optional). If you choose to request a confidential callback from the clinic, you may voluntarily provide your name, phone number, and email address, which is sent directly via WhatsApp to the clinic for the purpose of contacting you. This information is not stored by us.

Language Preference. We store your selected language (English or Urdu) locally on your device.

Advertising Data. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to calculate your assessment results, generate a supportive, non-diagnostic informational report via AI, facilitate an optional callback request with the clinic, remember your language preference, display relevant advertisements, and improve app stability.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your assessment responses are sent to Google's Gemini API solely to generate your informational report. If you request a callback, your contact details are sent directly to Dr. Muhammad Jahangeer Shah's clinic via WhatsApp for that sole purpose. We share data only with Google Gemini API, Google AdMob, and, only if you opt in, the clinic's WhatsApp contact.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your assessment responses and results are not stored on our servers after your report is generated. Callback request details are transmitted directly via WhatsApp and are not retained by us.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to use the app fully without providing any personal information beyond what is needed to generate your assessment, decline to submit a callback request, retake the assessment at any time, and opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for adult users and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Important Medical Disclaimer

Fertility & Wellness is an AI-based preliminary assessment tool that provides general educational and informational content only. It is not a medical diagnosis and is not a substitute for professional medical evaluation, diagnosis, or treatment. Herbal and lifestyle suggestions are general wellness information, not prescriptions. Always consult Dr. Muhammad Jahangeer Shah or a qualified healthcare provider regarding your fertility and reproductive health.

8. Third-Party Services

This app uses Google Gemini API and Google AdMob, each governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
