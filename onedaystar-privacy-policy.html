Privacy Policy — One Day Star

Last updated: August 2026

One Day Star ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Gameplay Information. When you use the app, you select a celebrity archetype, make choices throughout an interactive story, and optionally enter a name for your story. This information is used solely to generate your personalized story and is processed via a third-party AI service (Google Gemini API).

Story History. Stories you generate are saved locally on your device only. This data is not transmitted to our servers and is not accessible to us. You can delete individual stories or clear all history at any time within the app.

Advertising Data. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not require or collect any account information, email addresses, or personal contact details to use this app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to generate your personalized celebrity-day story via AI, display relevant advertisements, and improve app stability and performance.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The choices and name you enter during gameplay are sent to Google's Gemini API solely to generate your story text. We share data only with Google Gemini API (AI story generation) and Google AdMob (advertising).

4. Data Storage & Security

Your story history is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete this locally stored history.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to use the app fully without providing any personal information beyond an optional display name, delete individual stories or clear your entire story history at any time within the app, and opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

This app contains fictional roleplay scenarios written for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Content Disclaimer

One Day Star is a fictional entertainment experience. Celebrity archetypes featured in the app are inspired by public personalities and professions but do not represent real individuals, their actual views, or their endorsement of this app.

8. Third-Party Services

This app uses Google Gemini API and Google AdMob, each governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
