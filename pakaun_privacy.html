Privacy Policy — Aaj Kya Pakaun

Last updated: September 2026

Aaj Kya Pakaun ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Pantry Items. Ingredients you add to your pantry list are stored locally on your device only, using local device storage. This data is never transmitted to our servers.

Saved Recipes & Preferences. Recipes you save and your language preference are also stored locally on your device only.

Recipe Search Queries. If you search for a dish that isn't in our built-in recipe collection, your search text is sent to a third-party AI service (Google Gemini API) to generate a matching recipe. This is processed to generate your result and is not permanently stored on our servers.

Consultation Requests (Optional). If you choose to submit a non-emergency health question through the app's consultation form, the name and question you provide are used solely to facilitate a response and are not stored by us beyond that purpose.

Advertising Data. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not require account creation or collect email addresses to use this app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to match recipes to your pantry, generate AI recipes for dishes not in our collection, remember your saved recipes and language preference, facilitate optional consultation requests, and display relevant advertisements.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Recipe search queries are sent to Google's Gemini API solely to generate recipe content. We share data only with Google Gemini API (AI recipe generation) and Google AdMob (advertising).

4. Data Storage & Security

Your pantry list, saved recipes, and language preference are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to our servers. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete this locally stored data.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to use the app fully without providing any personal information, clear your pantry or saved recipes at any time within the app, and opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Health Content Disclaimer

Health and nutrition articles within this app provide general educational information only and are not a medical diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a licensed physician for personal medical concerns. The optional doctor consultation feature is for non-emergency questions only — for urgent symptoms, please seek immediate medical care.

8. AI-Generated Content Disclaimer

Recipes generated by AI for dishes not in our built-in collection have not been human-verified. Please use your own judgement, especially regarding cooking times and food safety.

9. Third-Party Services

This app uses Google Gemini API and Google AdMob, each governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
