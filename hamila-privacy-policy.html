Privacy Policy — Hamila Pregnancy Care

Last updated: August 2026

Hamila Pregnancy Care ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Account Information (Optional). Hamila works fully without creating an account. If you choose to create an optional account (to back up your data to the cloud), we collect your email address and a securely hashed password via Firebase Authentication.

Pregnancy & Health Information. If you use the app's tracking features, we may collect information you voluntarily enter, including your last menstrual period (LMP) date, weight, blood pressure, symptoms, baby kick counts, and your language preference. This information is stored locally on your device, and additionally in the cloud (Firebase/Firestore) only if you have created an optional account.

Advertising Data. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Usage & Analytics Data. We may collect general, non-personally-identifiable usage data (such as app crashes and feature usage) via Firebase to help us improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide and improve app features, sync your data across devices if you create an account, display relevant advertisements, diagnose technical issues, and communicate with you if you contact us for support.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with Google Firebase (authentication, cloud storage, analytics) and Google AdMob (advertising). These third-party services have their own privacy policies governing their use of your data.

4. Data Storage & Security

Data entered without an account stays on your device only and is not transmitted to our servers. If you create an account, your data is stored securely in Google Firebase's Firestore database with industry-standard security practices. You may delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to use the app without creating an account, request deletion of your account and associated data, request a copy of the data we hold about you, and opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for adult users (pregnant individuals and their partners) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Medical Disclaimer

Hamila Pregnancy Care provides general educational and informational content only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider regarding your pregnancy and any medical concerns.

8. Third-Party Services

This app uses Google Firebase (privacy policy) and Google AdMob (privacy policy), each with their own privacy policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
