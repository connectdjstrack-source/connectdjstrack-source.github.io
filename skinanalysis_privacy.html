Privacy Policy — Dr. Shah Skin Analysis

Last updated: September 2026

Dr. Shah Skin Analysis ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Skin Profile Information. When you use the skin analysis tool, the skin type, concerns, and lifestyle question answers you provide are sent to a third-party AI service (Google Gemini API) to generate a personalized, informational skin report. This data is processed to generate your result and is not permanently stored on our servers.

Lead Capture Information (Optional). If you choose to request a callback through the app's form, the name, phone number, and email you provide are used solely to facilitate a response and are sent directly via WhatsApp to the clinic. This information is not stored by us.

Advertising Data. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not require account creation and do not collect any photos or images of your skin — the analysis is based entirely on your self-reported answers to questions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to generate your personalized, AI-based skin analysis report, provide a recommended skincare routine, facilitate optional callback requests, and display relevant advertisements.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your skin profile answers are sent to Google's Gemini API solely to generate your informational report. If you request a callback, your contact details are sent directly to Dr. Muhammad Jahangeer Shah's clinic via WhatsApp for that sole purpose. We share data only with Google Gemini API, Google AdMob, and, only if you opt in, the clinic's WhatsApp contact.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your skin profile answers and generated report are not stored on our servers after being displayed to you. Callback request details are transmitted directly via WhatsApp and are not retained by us.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to use the app fully without providing any personal information beyond what is needed to generate your assessment, decline to submit a callback request, retake the analysis at any time, and opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Important Medical Disclaimer

Dr. Shah Skin Analysis is an AI-based preliminary assessment tool that provides general educational and informational content only. It is not a medical diagnosis and is not a substitute for professional dermatological evaluation, diagnosis, or treatment. Natural remedies and routine suggestions are general wellness information, not prescriptions. Always consult Dr. Muhammad Jahangeer Shah or a qualified healthcare provider regarding your skin health.

8. Third-Party Services

This app uses Google Gemini API and Google AdMob, each governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
